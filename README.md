# ♻️ ReLoop AI

> **Turning Industrial Waste into Intelligent Value**

ReLoop AI is a **100% software-based AI decision platform** built for **Project Jarvis – SU-05: AI-Based Industrial Waste Valorization**. It helps industries choose the most profitable and sustainable way to manage industrial waste by analyzing waste characteristics, market demand, logistics costs, and economic feasibility.

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

Industries generate different types of waste such as cotton scraps, plastic, cardboard, metal, wood, and used oil.

The challenge is **not identifying the waste**—it is deciding the best pathway for each waste type.

Factories often ask:

- Should this waste be reused?
- Should it be recycled?
- Is recovery more profitable?
- Will transport cost more than the waste's value?

These manual decisions lead to financial loss, unnecessary landfill waste, and missed recycling opportunities.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

ReLoop AI acts as an **AI Decision Engine**.

Instead of simply saying **"Recycle this"**, it recommends the most practical and profitable action by combining:

- Waste composition
- Quantity
- Purity
- Moisture
- Contamination
- Market demand
- Transport cost
- Processing cost
- Buyer availability

### Example Recommendation

**Input**

- Waste: Cotton Waste
- Quantity: 500 kg

**AI Output**

- Best Buyer: GreenTex Recycling
- Estimated Revenue: ₹8,500
- Estimated Profit: ₹6,000
- CO₂ Saved: 1.2 Tons

---

## 🚀 How It Works

1. Factory generates industrial waste.
2. Manager uploads waste details.
3. AI analyzes waste characteristics.
4. Market prices and buyer demand are checked.
5. Transport and processing costs are calculated.
6. AI recommends the best pathway.
7. Pickup is coordinated.
8. Dashboard and sustainability report are generated.

## 🌍 Innovation – EcoLink AI

Our signature innovation is **EcoLink AI**.

Instead of sending separate trucks from multiple nearby factories, EcoLink AI intelligently combines waste pickups.

### Example

| Factory | Plastic Waste |
|---------|--------------:|
| Factory A | 300 kg |
| Factory B | 250 kg |
| Factory C | 200 kg |

### Result

- One shared pickup
- Lower transport cost
- Higher profit
- Reduced CO₂ emissions

This transforms waste management into a collaborative logistics network.
